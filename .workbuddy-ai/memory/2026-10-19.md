### ★ The design insight that makes the build small
The invoker half splits in two: **holding** (`holdsCapability(person, feature)` — a feature-id
question, **no descriptor needed**) and **gating** (A0–A5 — needs one). `invokerCeilingFor` returns
early on `!capability` (`:495`), so holding is never asked for the 36. Asking it directly closes the
intersection rule for **all 157** without writing 36 descriptors.

slots 1–2** and welcome in slot 3, a **translation table as data**, and a **dated amendment log**.

**★ The best idea in it:** *jargon is a diagnostic, not a style fault.* If the sentence stating the
consequence needs a mechanism word, the consequence has not been decided yet. That converts an
unenforceable style rule into a thinking rule — and it is the same claim the note already makes about
diagrams.
